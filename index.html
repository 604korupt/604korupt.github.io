⚖️ Content Policy

Content Rules

Reuploading content without permission directly hurts creators. It steals views, divides audiences, and takes credit for work that took real time and effort to produce. I'm all for sharing my content, just do it the right way.

Please do not reupload my content without permission. I put serious time into every video. Unauthorized reuploads hurt the channel and the community I'm building. If you love the content, share the original link, that's the best support you can give.
01
Ask first. If you want to use my video, please contact me via email for permission before doing anything with it.
02
Credit me clearly. If permission is granted, you must credit me in both the title and description of the video, not just a small mention buried at the end.
03
No AI voiceovers or script recreation. Please do not use an AI voiceover to repeat what I've said or recreate the script in any form. This is a hard no.
04
Unauthorized reuploads will be removed. If my video is reuploaded without permission and proper credit, I will request removal through YouTube's standard process.
